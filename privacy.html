Axiom Flow ("the app") is operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME OR BUSINESS NAME], [CITY, PROVINCE], Canada ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices. It covers the website at finance.rahulchamp.ca and the Axiom Flow Android app, which displays the same service.

Who uses the app

Axiom Flow is a business tool for automotive dealership staff (finance managers, managers, salespeople and administrators). Accounts are created by a dealership administrator; the app is not intended for the general public or for children.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, work email or username, role, dealership, and account status.
  • Deal and delivery records entered by dealership staff: which may include a customer's name, vehicle details, finance or protection products offered and selected, pricing, and delivery progress.
  • Sign-in data: authentication tokens needed to keep you signed in, stored on your device.
  • Device preferences: settings such as light/dark theme and "remember me", stored locally on your device.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell personal information, and the app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you the data your role and dealership are allowed to see.
  • To let dealership staff prepare, present and track deals and vehicle deliveries.
  • To secure the service, troubleshoot problems and prevent misuse.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored with our hosting and database provider, Supabase, and the website is served through GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Dealership customer information

When dealership staff enter information about their customers, the dealership decides what is entered and why, and remains responsible for having the customer's consent. We process that information only to provide the service to the dealership.

Retention

We keep account and deal data while the dealership's account is active. When an account is deleted, the user's profile and sign-in are removed; deal and delivery records belong to the dealership and remain available to it, unassigned from the deleted user, unless the dealership asks us to delete them.

Your rights and account deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. To delete your account, follow the steps on our account deletion page. For privacy questions or complaints, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes
